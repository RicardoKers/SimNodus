# ADR 0099: Latches, flip-flops and counters are behavioral, with their state in capacitors

Date: 2026-10-19. Status: accepted (owner's request of 2026-10-18 and go-ahead
of 2026-10-19: latches, flip-flops and the other storage devices of
ngspice/XSPICE; requirement SIM-12, task SN-199).

## Context

ngspice 47 ships XSPICE digital code models in `digital.cm`: `d_dlatch`,
`d_srlatch`, `d_dff`, `d_jkff`, `d_tff`, `d_srff`, `d_ram` and others, joined
to analog nodes through `adc_bridge`/`dac_bridge`. It has no counter or
register model. Interactive runs restart ngspice for every segment (ADR 0080)
and carry only capacitor voltages and inductor currents.

Measurement (ngspice 47 console, `d_dff` as a toggle flip-flop, 1 kHz clock):

- Run in one piece it toggles correctly (5, 0, 5, 0 V after four edges).
- Its `ic` parameter sets the stored bit at the start: with the clock low at
  t = 0, `ic=1` gives Q = 5 V until the next edge, as wanted.
- With the clock **high** at t = 0 — what happens whenever a segment boundary
  falls in the clock's high phase — the model sees a rising edge at t = 0 and
  acts on it: Q was 0 V at 0.2 ms instead of the carried 5 V.

Carrying XSPICE state would therefore need reading every digital state at a
boundary, writing it back as `ic` and preventing the edge at t = 0, for which
the models offer nothing; it would also add `digital.cm` to the package.

A behavioral master-slave flip-flop, by contrast, keeps its bit in two small
capacitors, which the session already carries; a prototype toggled correctly at
the interactive step of 100 µs.

## Decision

- Storage parts are behavioral templates in the style of the logic gates
  (hidden supply VDD, inputs switch at VDD/2, outputs through about 10 ns of
  delay and 50 Ω): D latch, SR latch (reset wins), D, JK and T flip-flops
  (rising edge), a synchronous 4-bit counter and a 4-bit shift register (serial
  in, parallel out), both with an asynchronous clear. Category *Flip-flops &
  counters*; generated by `tools/generate_storage_parts.py`.
- A stored bit is the voltage of a 1 pF capacitor charged toward its input by a
  behavioral current source while enabled (1 ns time constant) and left alone
  otherwise. Edge-triggered parts are master-slave: the master follows its input
  while the clock is below 0.4 VDD, the slave copies it while the clock is above
  0.6 VDD, so the two are never open together.
- Everything starts with Q low. Q̅ and the clear inputs are *optional pins*
  (`"optional": true`): they may stay unconnected without a warning.
- XSPICE digital models are not used; memories (`d_ram`) are not offered, since
  their contents would not survive a segment and a memory has no circuit-level
  teaching use here yet.

## Alternatives

- **XSPICE models with `ic` carried per segment:** wrong state whenever a
  segment starts in the clock's high phase (measured above).
- **Flip-flops built from the existing gates by the user:** possible (and a good
  exercise with blocks, ADR 0098), but every feedback loop of gates costs
  nanosecond steps; one behavioral part is cheaper and exact at 100 µs.

## Consequences

- Measured: every part in a 18 ms transient; a counter over 17 interactive
  segments at a 40 Hz clock, 6257 samples, no wrong count.
- Timing is approximate like the gates: no setup/hold checks, no metastability;
  an input that changes exactly at a clock edge is taken or not depending on
  the step.
- Each stored bit adds two capacitors and two behavioral sources (one for a
  latch); a 4-bit counter is about 30 elements.
- Update (SN-213, the 555 timer): a latch with positive feedback inside an
  oscillator needs a master-slave pair. With one latch driving the discharge,
  ngspice's large steps settled on the oscillator's unstable middle point; and
  a segment starts from 0 V on nodes without a capacitor, so every decision
  waits for the supply. Segments now also start from the carried capacitor
  node voltages (`.ic`).

## Revisit criteria

- Larger digital designs (memories, wide registers) make the element count or
  speed a problem: then an event-driven digital engine beside ngspice is the
  candidate, not per-segment XSPICE state.
