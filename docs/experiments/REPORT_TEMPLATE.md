## Question and predeclared acceptance criteria

State the hypothesis, supported profile, reference, numerical/time tolerances, and why they are adequate. Do not choose tolerances after seeing the outcome.

## Environment and provenance

Host OS/architecture, compiler, CMake, backend/client/platform revisions, dependency source/checksums, firmware source/ELF hash, configuration, and licenses.

## Reproduction

Exact commands, input files, circuit/pin mapping, initialization, virtual-time settings, random seed if any, and expected runtime. Do not include secrets or personal absolute paths.

## Observations

Expected versus observed results, trace locations, units, actual stop/sample times, warnings, failure cases, and repeated-run behavior.

## Interpretation and limitations

Which requirement is supported by the evidence? What remains untested? Was coupling approximate? Were any models idealized?

## Decision and next step

Accept, revise, or reject the hypothesis. Link the ADR/backlog update and the smallest useful follow-up.
